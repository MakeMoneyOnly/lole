Lole is a restaurant operating system company inspired by Toast — the market leader
for restaurant technology in the US. Lole brings the same enterprise-grade reliability
to the Ethiopian and broader African market.

The organization is structured as a full executive corporation with a C-Suite, VP layer,
Engineering Managers, Product Managers, and 23 specialist Departments. Every agent
operates under a strict Heartbeat protocol and all decisions flow through the
6-stage deliberation pipeline.

**Tech Stack:** Next.js, Supabase, PowerSync, Apollo Router, CapacitorJS, Upstash.
**Compliance:** ERCA e-Invoice, MoR Fiscal API, Ethiopian tax law.
**Payments:** Chapa, Telebirr.
